# ANT: hold, buy or sell

Anthropic, PBC (Nasdaq: ANT). One share held. Prices as of 2026-11-20, about 11:35am Pacific. Not financial advice.

## Verdict

**Hold.** Don't buy more yet. Sell only if it closes clearly below $83, the IPO price.

## What the chart says

The whole first-day pop is gone in a day and a half. Most big IPOs that open hot do this: people who bought the open at $111 are underwater and sell into every bounce.

It stopped at $83.05, one nickel above the IPO price. In the first weeks the underwriters can buy stock to hold the offer price, so $83 is the first real floor. Since 11am it has bounced to about $86, the first sign of buyers there.

## Why hold

- **The floor is close.** From $86 to $83 is about 4%. That is a small risk on one share.
- **Trading one share costs too much.** On an $86 share, currency conversion and the spread would eat most of any short-term gain.
- **Two days of chart don't change the bet.** If you bought it as a long-term bet on the company, nothing in two days of trading changes that.

## Why not buy more yet

- **No signal to trade.** Our strategies (Double 7s, RSI(2), trend; see [WHITEPAPER.md](../WHITEPAPER.md)) read the 200-day average, and ANT has two days of history.
- **Lots of shares for sale.** 980M new shares just hit the market. When the lockup ends, usually about 180 days after the IPO (the terms aren't confirmed), insiders can sell too.
- **Losses and mood.** A $23B quarterly loss, and investors have cooled on AI stocks.

## Why not sell

Selling now means selling right on the floor, after a two-day drop. If $83 holds, this is the worst price of the move.

## The rules

1. **Sell** if it closes below $83 two days in a row. The floor broke.
2. **Buy more** if it closes back above $97.17 (the day-1 close) on heavy volume, or holds between $83 and $90 for a week or more.
3. **Otherwise do nothing.** Check it once a week, and again before the lockup ends.
