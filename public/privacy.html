Privacy Policy

Epiphany, on the web at epiphany.jaybulb.com and on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch. Last updated October 21, 2026.

Epiphany is made by one person, Joshua Trommel, in British Columbia, Canada. This page says what the app collects, why, who else touches it, and how to get it deleted. It follows Canada's privacy law (PIPEDA).

What we collect

  • Your account. Your email address, your name if you give it, and a password stored only as a hash. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we get the email and name they share and nothing else.
  • Your money. Holdings, accounts, budget, income, debt and goals that you type in, and statements you upload. Uploaded statements are read for their transactions and the results are saved to your account.
  • A brokerage, if you link one. Holdings and balances, read only. The connection runs through SnapTrade or the brokerage itself. We never see or store your brokerage password.
  • Location, if you allow it. Your approximate location shows what is around you on the map. It is sent to the public data sources listed below as coordinates, and it is not saved to your account or kept as a history.
  • People you look up. Names you search in People are sent to a search provider to find public information. People you save, with your tags and notes, are stored in your account.
  • A profile photo, if you set one.
  • Purchases. Whether you have paid. On the web, Stripe handles the card and we never see the number. In the apps, Apple handles the purchase.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers.
  • There are no ads, no ad trackers and no cross-app tracking.
  • Nothing in Epiphany is investment advice, and we don't place trades unless you turn that on yourself.

Who else touches your data

  • Cloudflare runs the website and the server.
  • Upstash stores your account and portfolio data.
  • SnapTrade connects a brokerage when you link one.
  • Stripe takes payments on the web. Apple takes payments in the apps.
  • Apple and Google handle their sign-in buttons.
  • Resend sends account emails, like password resets.
  • Public data sources such as OpenStreetMap, Yelp, USGS, weather services and flight trackers get map coordinates. Market and news providers such as Yahoo Finance get the symbols you look at. Google, DuckDuckGo and Wikipedia get People searches. None of them get your name or email from us.

Cookies

The website uses one cookie to keep you signed in. There are no analytics or advertising cookies.

Keeping and deleting it

Your data stays while your account exists. You can delete your account and everything in it at any time: in the iPhone and iPad app from Settings, then Delete Account, or from anywhere by emailing us. Deleted data is gone from our systems within 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to fix it, or ask us to delete it. Email us and you will get an answer within 30 days. If you are not happy with the answer, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at priv.gc.ca.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Big changes will be called out in the app.
