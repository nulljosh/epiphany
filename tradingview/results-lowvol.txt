LOW VOLATILITY on Ken French tenths (value weighted, survivorship free): lowest variance tenth and lowest beta tenth of all US stocks, long only, monthly.
French files used: Portfolios_Formed_on_VAR (10 Portfolios Formed on Variance: total return variance over the last 60 days, re-sorted every month end) and
  Portfolios_Formed_on_BETA (10 Portfolios Formed on Beta: Scholes-Williams beta on 60 months, re-sorted every June). Both start 1963-07, so the unseen half is 1976-now and the
  'train' stretch 1963-07 to 1975-12 is only 12.5 years. Nothing is tuned anywhere, so train is just one more window, not a tuning set. Plain quality (top operating profit tenth) is the in-house yardstick.
Costs: every tenth 30% yearly one way turnover at 0.1% a side = 0.06% a year. The variance tenth is re-sorted monthly, so its real turnover is surely higher than a yearly sort: the '100% turnover' column
  charges 100% a year (0.2% a year) as a sensitivity, not a pick. Stress = 0.5% a side. Trend filter = hold the tenth only while the MARKET (Mkt-RF + RF cumulative) is above its 10 month
  average, else T-bills (French RF); month end t decides month t+1; 0.1% fee on each switch; no leverage. Late = signal acts one month later.
1.5x rows (1.5x fixed from Frazzini and Pedersen's logic, not tuned): 1.5 x tenth return - 0.5 x (T-bill + 1% a year margin), turnover cost x1.5, re-levered to 1.5x every month end paying 0.1% a side on the dollars moved. No fund fee (margin on the tenth itself).
  Monthly resolution misses intramonth drops, so every worst drop reads a little kinder than a daily one. Sharpe = excess over French RF, annualised, blind.
