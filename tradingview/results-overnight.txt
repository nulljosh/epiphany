OVERNIGHT VS INTRADAY, SPY daily open and close, 1993-01-29 to 2026-10-01 (today's unfinished session dropped), Yahoo adjusted (dividends in; a dividend goes to the overnight leg, the ex-date open is where it drops).
Context (train) 1993-01-29..2009-12-31, BLIND 2010-01-04..2026-10-01 (4212 sessions, 16.8 years). Nothing is tuned: three fixed rules, one 200 day filter, fees read off a ladder.
Night = close j-1 to open j (weekends and holidays included). Session = open j to close j. Overnight leg compounds the nights, intraday leg the sessions; together they are exactly the buy and hold total return.
Strategies: (a) in SPY every night, bills in the session; (b) in SPY every session, bills overnight; (c) (a) only when SPY closed above its 200 day average two closes ago (house timing: read j-2, trade j-1).
Cash earns the 3 month bill rate (FRED), 27% of a day in the session and the rest overnight. Two trades a day for (a) and (b): buy then sell, a fee on each side, a side = fee x notional. SPY hold pays nothing.
IBKR level: Pro Fixed $0.005 a share, $1 minimum, on a $100,000 sleeve at the day's real SPY price (shares = sleeve / price), plus half the spread (0.01%) a side. Exchange, clearing and SEC/FINRA sell fees are not modelled.
