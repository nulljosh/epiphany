daily-reset 2x fund model with 0.9% expense, 0.8% a year model gap taken off). Pick on 1929-01-02..1975-12-31, blind 1976-01-02..2026-10-01.
Out-assets, daily, net of fund expenses (IEF 0.15%, TLT 0.15%, GLD 0.40%). IEF-like = 10y Treasury priced off GS10 yields. TLT-like = 20y par bond
repriced off the same GS10 yield (parallel shift, 10y coupon). Gold = monthly price series (fixed at $20.67/$35 until 1971, private ownership banned
until 1974, so its train half is not a real test) spread over the month. 2x bond = daily-reset 2x of that bond, T-bill financing, 0.9% expense + gap.
'own trend' = hold it only while its own total return index is above its 200 day average, else bills (signal timing as the stock rule).
The 50/50 mix is half bills, half gold, rebalance cost ignored. Fee counted on every non-cash dollar bought or sold.
