SINGLE ASSET TREND FOLLOWING, 1x, long only. Fixed rule (Faber 2007), nothing tuned, nothing picked: hold the asset while it closes above its
10 month average (monthly century series) or 200 day average (daily funds), else T-bills. 0.1% per switch. Daily: signal at close j-2, trade close j-1, earn day j.
Monthly: signal at month end t-1 earns month t. Columns: TREND CAGR/worst drop, HOLD the asset, T-bills CAGR, decades the trend beat holding, calendar 2008 and 2022 (return/drop),
'beats' = share of 300 random in/out schedules (same days in the market, same number of switches) the trend CAGR beats, '=' = randoms matching on CAGR and drop,
late = signal acted on 1 month late, in = share of time in the asset, sw/y = switches a year. Bar: more CAGR than holding, no bigger worst drop, most decades won.
