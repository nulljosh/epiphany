QUALITY UPGRADES on Ken French monthly tenths (value weighted, survivorship free). Quality = top operating profitability tenth.
Blind 1976-01 to 2026-08. Every rule is fixed in advance, nothing picked. French has no operating profitability x momentum portfolio file, so the blends are 50/50 of two tenths, rebalanced monthly.
Costs: quality and value tenths 30% yearly one way turnover at 0.1% a side = 0.06% a year; momentum tenth 100% a month traded = 0.1% a month;
  blends also pay 0.1% a side on the dollars moved to rebalance back to 50/50 each month end. Trend filter = hold the sleeve only while the MARKET (Mkt-RF + RF cumulative) is above its
  10 month average, else T-bills (French RF); month end t decides month t+1; 0.1% fee on each switch; no leverage. Stress = 0.5% a side on everything incl. switches. Late = signal acts one month later.
  Monthly resolution misses intramonth drops, so every worst drop reads a little kinder than a daily one.
