CROSS-ASSET DUAL MOMENTUM, TREND FILTER, LEVERAGE. Daily engine on the --out series: S&P 500 with dividends (stocks), 10y Treasury priced off GS10 (bonds, IEF 0.15% off),
gold monthly price spread over the days (GLD 0.40% off), T-bills. Signal at each month end, traded at the next day's close, earns from the day after (first close of next month).
0.1% a switch on every non-cash dollar bought or sold. 2x = daily-reset 2x fund model, T-bill financing, 0.9% expense, 0.8% a year model gap taken off.
Fixed from the literature, nothing tuned: 12 month lookback, 10 month average. Train 1929-01-02..1975-12-31, BLIND 1976-01-02..2026-10-01.
Gold is a fixed price (one revaluation, Jan 1934, +69%) until 1971 and private gold was banned until 1974, so the train half of any gold row is not tradable history.
Plain stocks carry no expense in any row, as in the S&P hold rows.
