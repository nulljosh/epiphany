4. EXPECTED FALSE PASSES
   Null pass rate p0 = chance a rule with NO true edge clears the 3 part bar (more return, no bigger drop, most decades).
   Measured by Monte Carlo: demean each winner's monthly excess over hold, resample in 12 month blocks, add to the real
   holding path, test the bar. 4000 draws each. The random schedule baselines in results-leverage-robust.txt,
   results-quality.txt and friends say the same thing from the other side: random in and out timing almost never
   matched a winner on return and drop (0 of 1000 for Trend 2x).
   Trend 2x S&P: null pass rate 0.034
   Quality tenth (French): null pass rate 0.106
   Average p0 = 0.070. Observed passes: 2 (Trend 2x S&P and Trend 2x Nasdaq 100, the same bet), plus Quality (vs hold only, and the real fund fails).
   all trials N  : expected false passes 10.3; P(at least 2 by luck) 1.00; P(at least 3) 1.00
   40 effective  : expected false passes 2.8; P(at least 2 by luck) 0.77; P(at least 3) 0.53
   10 effective  : expected false passes 0.7; P(at least 2 by luck) 0.15; P(at least 3) 0.03
   Read: noise alone clears this bar about 3% to 11% of the time for rules shaped like our winners. Over the independent
   bets (somewhere between 10 and 40) that is 1 to 3 false passes. We got 2 or 3. That is what luck looks like.
   If all 148 trials were independent the luck count would be 10, and the observed 2 or 3 would sit below it, but they are
   not independent, most are the same trend rule on the same index, so the effective number is the honest one to use.
