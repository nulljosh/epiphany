KEN FRENCH LONG-ONLY FACTOR TILTS. Monthly, value weighted, survivorship free (CRSP). Top or bottom tenth of all US stocks sorted on each trait.
Market = Mkt-RF + RF. Blind 1976-01 to 2026-08. No settings were picked: every rule below is fixed in advance, so there is no train half to tune on.
Costs: value, size, quality, investment rebalance once a year in the French construction, assumed 30% yearly turnover, charged 0.1% a side
  = 0.06% a year. Momentum keeps the existing 100% a month turnover, 0.1% a month. Stress row: 0.5% a side on the same turnover.
Stack = hold the tenth at 2x only while the MARKET is above its 10 month average (month-end levels, decided at month end, earns the next month), else T-bills (French RF).
  2x month = 2 x tenth return - RF - 0.9%/12 expense - 0.8%/12 real-fund gap. Costs double at 2x. 0.1% fee on every switch. This is monthly and coarser than a daily-reset fund.
  Monthly resolution misses intramonth drops, so every worst drop here reads a little kinder than a daily one.
