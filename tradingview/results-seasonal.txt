CALENDAR OVERLAYS. Daily, 1929-01-02 to 2026-10-01. Context (train) 1929-01-02..1975-12-31, blind 1976-01-02..2026-10-01. Nothing is tuned: every window is fixed from the paper it comes from.
Fund model as Trend 2x: S&P with dividends, 2x fund = daily reset, T-bill financing, 0.9% expense, 0.8% a year model gap taken off; plain fund 0.09%; bills when out.
Fee 0.1% on every non-cash dollar traded, so a swap between the 1x and 2x fund (sell one, buy the other) pays it twice; fee sensitivity below. 'Trend' = 2x above the S&P 200 day average
(signal read at close j-2, traded close j-1, earns day j). Calendar windows are known ahead and need no signal lag.
Windows: tom = last trading day of the month plus the first 3 (Lakonishok and Smidt 1988); halloween = in Nov to Apr, out May to Oct (Bouman and Jacobsen 2002);
preholiday = the last trading day before any gap of 2 or more weekdays in the price series (Ariel 1990, an approximation of NYSE holidays); nomonday = out Friday close to Monday close.
Stacks always keep the trend gate: filter = Trend 2x inside the window, Trend 1x outside it; skip = Trend 2x, bills when the window is off. 'alone' = 1x S&P in the window, bills out, no trend.
Days a year in the window, blind: tom 48, halloween 124, preholiday 9, nomonday 204   (252 trading days a year). Pre-holiday days found in 2024: 01-12, 02-16, 03-28, 05-24, 06-18, 07-03, 08-30, 11-27, 12-24, 12-31.

Check: this engine's Trend 2x blind 14.4% / 44.0% matches lev_sim (14.4% / 44.0%).
Baselines. Standalone vs S&P hold: train 7.7% / 84%, BLIND 12.1% / 55.3% (no fees, no expense; the overlays pay the 0.09% fund expense, so they are a hair worse off).
Stacked vs Trend 2x: train 14.0% / 80%, BLIND 14.4% / 44.0%.
